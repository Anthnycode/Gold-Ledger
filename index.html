How it's calculated 
per gram = metal price ÷ 31.1035 × purity
purity   = gold: hallmark fineness for whole
           karats (14K = .585), else karat ÷ 24
           silver/platinum: fineness ÷ 1000
           (925 = .925)
per gram can also be typed in by hand
total    = weight (g) × per gram
profit   = total − Sierra − Princess
